How a shoot
runs

Working with Hitmark is meant to be the start of a long relationship. We choose projects carefully, keep you inside every step, and deliver fast enough that the audience still cares.

01

Research

We ask questions before we quote. What the film has to do, who it is for, where it will run, what success looks like a month after delivery.

02

Preparation

Flight plans, permits, airspace, shot lists and call sheets, shared with you. You are never out of the loop; this is your project and we are here to make it better.

03

Execution

The latest gear, a crew that has flown together, and a day that runs clean, swift and professional while everything unfolds into something memorable.

04

Delivery

Our reputation lives in the turnaround. Event recaps overnight, campaign films on schedule, with social cutdowns the audience can share now.
